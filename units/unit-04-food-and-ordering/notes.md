### Taiwan-friendly Notes 台灣用法補充

- `cuisine` 比 `food` 更像「某地料理」：Japanese cuisine, Thai cuisine, Taiwanese cuisine。
- `dish` 是「一道菜」，不是「盤子」。`My favorite dish is beef noodle soup.`
- `sweet tooth` 是「愛吃甜食的人」：I have a sweet tooth.
- `portion` 指份量，不是「部分」而已：The portion is too large for me.
- `recommend` 後面直接接名詞很自然：I recommend the grilled chicken.

## Useful Patterns 句型與文法重點

### 1. Talking About Preferences 表達食物偏好

```text
I like / love + food.
I really like + cuisine, especially + adjective + dishes.
I prefer + noun.
```

Examples:

- I really like Asian cuisine, especially spicy dishes.
- I love Thai food because the flavors are amazing.
- I prefer something healthy, like grilled chicken and vegetables.

Socratic prompt:

> What kind of food do you like? Can you add one reason?

---

### 2. Talking About Taste 描述味道

```text
This dish is + adjective.
This food tastes + adjective.
The flavor is + adjective.
```

Examples:

- This dish is fresh and delicious.
- The soup tastes sour.
- The fries are too salty.

Watch out:

- Natural: The soup tastes sour.
- Less natural: The soup is sour taste.

---

### 3. Giving Opinions 表達看法

```text
I think + sentence.
In my opinion, + sentence.
```

Examples:

- I think fast food is convenient but unhealthy.
- In my opinion, a balanced diet is important.

Better speaking move:

```text
I think fast food is useful when people are busy, but we should not eat it too often.
```

---

### 4. Making Suggestions 推薦與建議

```text
You should try + food.
I recommend + noun.
What do you recommend?
Do you have any recommendations?
```

Examples:

- You should try the soup.
- I recommend the grilled chicken.
- What do you recommend for a first-time customer?

Watch out:

- Natural: I recommend this dish.
- Natural: Could you recommend something popular?
- Less natural: I recommend you this dish.

---

### 5. Talking About Habits 描述飲食習慣

```text
I usually eat + food.
I usually order + food.
I try to + verb.
```

Examples:

- I usually eat rice for lunch.
- I usually order spicy noodles or fried rice.
- I try to eat healthy food.

Use this for your own answer:

```text
I usually eat ___ for breakfast. For lunch, I often ___. I try to ___ because ___.
```

---

### 6. Polite Ordering 禮貌點餐

```text
I'd like to + verb.
I'd like + item.
Could I have + item?
Can I get + extra item?
```

Examples:

- I'd like to order now.
- I'd like a combo meal with a burger, fries, and a soft drink.
- Could I have a glass of water?
- Can I get extra sauce?

Why this matters:

中文說「我要一份套餐」很自然，但英文餐廳裡直接說 `I want...` 有時聽起來比較硬。`I'd like...` 和 `Could I have...?` 更禮貌，也更像真實服務情境。

---

### 7. Asking About Ingredients 詢問食材

```text
What's in + dish?
What ingredients are in + dish?
Does it have + ingredient?
```

Examples:

- What's in this pasta?
- What ingredients are in this dish?
- Does it have peanuts? I have a peanut allergy.

---

### 8. Making Requests 提出需求

```text
Could you + verb, please?
Could you make it less spicy, please?
Could we get + item?
```

Examples:

- Could you make it less spicy, please?
- Could we get a side salad?
- Could you change this dish?

---

### 9. Handling Problems / Complaints 處理餐點問題

```text
Excuse me, this dish is a bit + problem.
I'm afraid this is + problem.
Could you change it?
Could you replace it?
```

Examples:

- Excuse me, this dish is a bit cold.
- I'm afraid this is too salty.
- Could you replace it, please?

Tone note:

`I'm afraid...` 不是「我害怕」，在這裡是禮貌地提出不太好的消息，語氣比直接說 `This is wrong` 更柔和。

---

### 10. Paying 結帳

```text
Could we have the bill, please?
Could we have the check, please?
Can I pay by card?
```

Examples:

- Could we have the bill, please?
- Can I pay by card?
- Should we pay at the cashier?

## Reading Notes 閱讀重點

### Healthy Eating Habits

Healthy eating is important for both the body and the mind. Junk food like burgers, fries, and sugary drinks may taste good, but it is not very healthy. A healthy diet includes vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and protein. Some people avoid certain ingredients because of allergies, and vegetarians do not eat meat. Eating habits differ around the world, but a balanced diet is important everywhere.

Key ideas:

- Junk food tastes good but can be unhealthy.
- Whole grains, fruit, vegetables, and protein help us stay strong.
- Allergies and vegetarian diets affect food choices.
- A balanced diet matters no matter where you live.

### Fast Food and Modern Lifestyles

Fast food is popular because it is quick, affordable, and easy to find. It is convenient for busy people, especially in cities. However, fast food is often high in fat, sugar, and salt, so eating it too often can cause health problems. Some fast food chains now offer healthier options, such as salads, fruit, and low-calorie meals. The best approach is to make balanced choices and eat fast food in moderation.

Key ideas:

- Fast food is convenient and popular.
- Common fast food includes burgers, fries, pizza, and fried chicken.
- Eating too much fast food can be unhealthy.
- Some restaurants now offer healthier options.
- Moderation and balance are the main message.

## Comprehension Practice 閱讀理解練習

請先用英文回答，再看提示。

1. Why is healthy eating important?
   - Hint: Think about `body`, `mind`, and `energy`.
2. What are examples of junk food?
   - Hint: Name at least three foods or drinks.
3. What foods are part of a healthy diet?
   - Hint: Use a list with vegetables, fruits, whole grains, and protein.
4. What do vegetarians avoid?
   - Hint: Use `avoid + noun`.
5. Why do people choose fast food?
   - Hint: Use three adjectives: quick, affordable, convenient.
6. What are the disadvantages of fast food?
   - Hint: Use `high in fat, sugar, and salt`.
7. How can people eat fast food in a healthy way?
   - Hint: Use `in moderation` and `balanced choices`.

## Socratic Practice 蘇格拉底式練習

請不要先背答案。先用英文講出你的版本，再用提示把句子升級。

1. What is your favorite food?
   - Starter: My favorite food is...
   - Upgrade: Add ingredients, taste, and why you like it.
2. Do you prefer spicy or sweet food? Why?
   - Starter: I prefer...
   - Upgrade: Compare two tastes and give one example.
3. How often do you eat fast food?
   - Starter: I eat fast food...
   - Upgrade: Add whether you think the habit is healthy.
4. What do you usually order at a restaurant?
   - Starter: I usually order...
   - Upgrade: Add cuisine and a side dish or beverage.
5. Have you ever had a bad experience in a restaurant?
   - Starter: Yes, once...
   - Upgrade: Use a polite complaint sentence.
6. Do you think fast food is necessary in modern life?
   - Starter: I think it is / it isn't necessary because...
   - Upgrade: Mention busy people, convenience, and moderation.
7. If you have a food allergy, what should you ask?
   - Starter: Does this dish have...?
   - Upgrade: Add `I have a ___ allergy.`

## Common Mistakes 我的常犯錯提醒

| Original Pattern | Better Pattern | Why |
|------------------|----------------|-----|
| I want a combo meal. | I'd like a combo meal, please. | 餐廳點餐用 `I'd like...` 更禮貌。 |
| I recommend you this dish. | I recommend this dish. | `recommend` 後面可直接接推薦項目；不要直翻中文「推薦你」。 |
| This soup is sour taste. | This soup tastes sour. | 描述味道用 `taste + adjective`。 |
| I like eat sushi. | I like eating sushi. / I like to eat sushi. | `like` 後面接 V-ing 或 to + V。 |
| I usually order Sushi. | I usually order sushi. | 一般食物名稱不用大寫，專有名詞如 Japanese 才大寫。 |
| I don't have allergy. | I don't have any allergies. | `allergy` 是可數名詞，否定常用複數 `any allergies`。 |
| We will go to my mother-in-law's house. | We're going to my mother-in-law's house. | 已安排好的近期計畫，口語常用現在進行式。 |
| take away | takeaway / to go / takeout | 在餐廳說外帶，美式常用 `to go`，英式常見 `takeaway`。 |
| dine in = eat at the restaurant | dine in = 內用 | 台灣情境用「內用」比較自然。 |

## Role-play 情境模擬

### Situation A: Food Preferences

**Teacher:** What kind of food do you like?  
**Student:** I really like Asian cuisine, especially spicy dishes.  
**Teacher:** Do you like Thai food?  
**Student:** Yes, I love it. The flavors are amazing.  
**Teacher:** What do you usually order?  
**Student:** I usually order spicy noodles or fried rice. How about you?  
**Teacher:** I prefer something healthy, like grilled chicken and vegetables.  
**Student:** That sounds good. Do you eat junk food?  
**Teacher:** Sometimes, but not often. I try to eat healthy food.  
**Student:** Same here. The portion sizes are usually too big in fast food restaurants.  
**Teacher:** Yes, and they are often too salty.  
**Student:** Do you have a favorite dish?  
**Teacher:** Yes, I love sushi. It's fresh and delicious.  
**Student:** I've never tried it. Do you recommend it?  
**Teacher:** Definitely! You should try it someday.

Practice version:

- Replace `Asian cuisine` with a cuisine you like.
- Replace `spicy noodles or fried rice` with your real order.
- Add one sentence about why you like that food.

### Situation B: At a Restaurant

**Waiter:** Good evening. Welcome! Do you have a reservation?  
**Customer:** Yes, it's under the name Chen.  
**Waiter:** Right this way. Here are your menus.  
**Customer:** Thank you.  
**Waiter:** Are you ready to order?  
**Customer:** Not yet. Could you recommend something popular?  
**Waiter:** Our grilled chicken with vegetables is very popular.  
**Customer:** That sounds good. What ingredients does it have?  
**Waiter:** It includes chicken, fresh vegetables, and a light sauce.  
**Customer:** Great. I'll have that, please.  
**Waiter:** And for you?  
**Customer 2:** I'd like a combo meal with a burger, fries, and a soft drink.  
**Waiter:** What would you like to drink?  
**Customer 2:** A cola, please.  
**Waiter:** Would you like anything else?  
**Customer:** Yes, could we get a side salad?  
**Waiter:** Of course. Is everything for dine-in?  
**Customer:** Yes, it is.  
**Customer:** Excuse me, this dish is a bit cold.  
**Waiter:** I'm very sorry. I'll replace it right away.  
**Customer:** Could we have the bill, please?  
**Waiter:** Certainly. Here you are.

Practice version:

- Change the reservation name.
- Ask one allergy question.
- Make one request, such as less spicy, no onions, or extra sauce.
- Politely complain about one problem.

## Speaking Templates 口說模板

### My Favorite Food

```text
My favorite food is ___.
It has ___, ___, and ___.
It tastes ___ and ___.
I like it because ___.
I usually eat it when ___.
```

Example:

```text
My favorite food is beef noodle soup.
It has beef, noodles, vegetables, and soup.
It tastes savory and a little spicy.
I like it because it is warm and filling.
I usually eat it on weekends.
```

### My Eating Habits

```text
I usually eat ___ for breakfast.
For lunch, I often eat ___.
I try to eat healthy food, but sometimes I eat ___.
I think my diet is ___ because ___.
In the future, I want to ___.
```

### Fast Food Opinion

```text
In my opinion, fast food is ___.
Many people choose it because ___.
However, it can be unhealthy because ___.
I think people should ___.
```
