## Useful Patterns 句型與文法重點

### Describe personality

```text
I am + adjective.
He/She is + adjective.
My friend is + adjective and adjective.
```

Examples:

- I am helpful and reliable at work.
- My wife is cheerful and easygoing.
- My coworker is intelligent, serious, and reliable.

### Give reasons

```text
I think he/she is reliable because...
I like friends who are honest because...
This personality is important because...
```

Examples:

- I am reliable at work because I always finish my tasks on time.
- I like friends who are honest because I can trust them.
- Being sociable is useful because it helps people communicate.

### Compare personality

```text
I am more easygoing than serious.
I prefer sociable people because...
```

Better sentence:

- I am more easygoing than serious because I like a relaxed atmosphere.

Watch out:

- 不自然：I am more easygoing person.
- 較自然：I am more of an easygoing person.

## Socratic Practice 蘇格拉底式練習

請先用英文回答，再看提示修正：

1. How would you describe your personality?
   - Hint: Use at least 3 adjectives and one reason.
2. What personality traits do you like in friends?
   - Hint: Try `I like friends who are... because...`
3. Are you more sociable or serious?
   - Hint: Compare two traits and explain when each one appears.
4. When do you usually feel nervous?
   - Hint: Use a time phrase: `before`, `during`, or `when`.
5. Which personality trait do you want to improve?
   - Hint: Connect it to work, family, or English learning.

## Common Mistakes 我的常犯錯提醒

| Original Pattern | Better Pattern | Why |
|------------------|----------------|-----|
| I am more easygoing person. | I am more of an easygoing person. | 單數可數名詞 `person` 前需要冠詞，也要用更自然的比較說法。 |
| too much serious is boring | being too serious is boring | `serious` 是形容詞，當主詞時可用 `being + adjective`。 |
| I am intelligent with software engineering. | I am good at software engineering. | `good at` 用來描述擅長某領域。 |
| during job interview | during a job interview | 單數可數名詞 `interview` 前要加 `a`。 |
| reliable friend because I don't lie | I am a reliable friend because I don't lie. | 英文完整句需要主詞與 be 動詞。 |

## Role-play 情境模擬

**Situation:** Two coworkers are talking about a new team member.

**Anna:** Hey David, have you met the new employee, Kevin?

**David:** Yes, I talked to him yesterday. He seems very intelligent and polite.

**Anna:** I agree. I also think he is very sociable. During lunch, he talked with everyone in the office.

**David:** Yes, I noticed that too. It is good to have someone friendly on the team.

**Anna:** Do you think he will work well with us?

**David:** I think so. He seems reliable and hardworking. He said he likes helping coworkers.

**Anna:** That is great. A helpful colleague makes a big difference.

**David:** I also noticed Kevin was a little nervous on his first day.

**Anna:** That is normal. He also seems brave enough to ask questions.

## Reading Notes 閱讀重點

Personality affects workplace communication, teamwork, productivity, and leadership.

- Sociable people help create a friendly work environment.
- Easygoing employees stay calm in stressful situations.
- Serious workers focus on tasks and responsibilities.
- Reliable employees are valuable because teammates can trust them.
- Brave, honest, and generous leaders can gain trust and respect.
- Selfish or nervous traits may create communication problems if they are not managed.
