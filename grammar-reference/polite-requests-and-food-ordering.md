# Polite Requests & Food Ordering 禮貌請求與餐廳點餐

> Use these patterns when ordering food, asking for information, making requests, handling problems, and paying.

---

## 1. Ordering Politely 禮貌點餐

### Core Patterns

```text
I'd like + item, please.
I'd like to + verb.
Could I have + item?
Can I get + item?
```

### Examples

| Direct / Less Polite | Better |
|----------------------|--------|
| I want a burger. | I'd like a burger, please. |
| Give me water. | Could I have a glass of water? |
| I want to order. | I'd like to order now. |
| I need sauce. | Can I get extra sauce? |

### Taiwan Note

中文點餐常說「我要...」，但英文餐廳情境裡，`I'd like...` 比 `I want...` 更自然也更禮貌。

---

## 2. Asking For Recommendations 詢問推薦

```text
What do you recommend?
Could you recommend something popular?
Do you have any recommendations?
What do you recommend for a first-time customer?
```

### Examples

- What do you recommend?
- Could you recommend something popular?
- Do you have any recommendations for vegetarians?

### Common Mistake

| Wrong | Better | Why |
|-------|--------|-----|
| I recommend you this dish. | I recommend this dish. | `recommend` 可以直接接推薦項目。 |
| Can you recommend me food? | Could you recommend something? | 更自然，也比較有禮貌。 |

---

## 3. Asking About Ingredients 詢問食材

```text
What's in + dish?
What ingredients are in + dish?
Does it have + ingredient?
```

### Examples

- What's in this pasta?
- What ingredients are in this dish?
- Does this dish have peanuts? I have a peanut allergy.

### Allergy Patterns

```text
I have a + food + allergy.
I don't have any allergies.
I'm allergic to + food.
```

Examples:

- I have a peanut allergy.
- I don't have any allergies.
- I'm allergic to shellfish.

---

## 4. Making Special Requests 提出特殊需求

```text
Could you make it less spicy, please?
Could you make it without onions?
Can I get extra sauce?
Could we get a side salad?
```

### Useful Food Request Words

| Request | 中文 | Example |
|---------|------|---------|
| less spicy | 少辣一點 | Could you make it less spicy? |
| no onions | 不要洋蔥 | Could you make it without onions? |
| extra sauce | 多一點醬 | Can I get extra sauce? |
| side dish | 配菜 | Could we get a side dish? |
| refill | 續杯 | Can I get a refill? |

---

## 5. Handling Problems / Complaints 處理餐點問題

### Polite Complaint Patterns

```text
Excuse me, this dish is a bit + problem.
I'm afraid this is + problem.
Could you change it?
Could you replace it?
```

### Examples

- Excuse me, this dish is a bit cold.
- I'm afraid this is too salty.
- Could you replace it, please?

### Tone Note

`I'm afraid...` 在這裡不是「我害怕」，而是禮貌地提出問題，像中文的「不好意思，這個可能...」。

---

## 6. Dine In, Takeaway, To Go 內用與外帶

| Phrase | 中文 | Note |
|--------|------|------|
| dine in | 內用 | We prefer to dine in. |
| to go | 外帶 | I'd like this to go. |
| takeout | 外帶 | Common in American English. |
| takeaway | 外帶 | Common in British English. |

### Examples

- Is this for here or to go?
- We'd like to dine in.
- I'd like this to go, please.

---

## 7. Paying 結帳

```text
Could we have the bill, please?
Could we have the check, please?
Can I pay by card?
Should we pay at the cashier?
```

### Examples

- Could we have the bill, please?
- Can I pay by card?
- Should we pay at the cashier?

---

## Quick Self-check 自我檢查

Before speaking in a restaurant, ask yourself:

1. Did I use `I'd like` or `Could I have`?
2. Did I add `please`?
3. If I have an allergy, did I ask about ingredients?
4. If there is a problem, did I start politely with `Excuse me` or `I'm afraid`?
5. If I want takeaway, did I say `to go`?
